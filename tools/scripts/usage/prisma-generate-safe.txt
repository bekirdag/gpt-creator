Usage: prisma-generate-safe.sh [project-root] [-- prisma-cli-args...]

Runs `prisma generate` with workspace-independent safeguards:
  • Ensures Prisma engine binaries are written to a writable cache
  • Falls back across pnpm, npx, and globally installed prisma CLIs
  • Ignores Node engine pinning when pnpm runs with a mismatched runtime

Examples:
  scripts/prisma-generate-safe.sh
  scripts/prisma-generate-safe.sh /path/to/project
  scripts/prisma-generate-safe.sh . -- --schema apps/api/prisma/schema.prisma
